# Automated Morning Briefing — Claude Code Spec

## What This Is
A daily morning briefing delivered by 8:00 AM PT via email. It should read like a sharp, conversational newsletter written by someone who deeply understands my role, industry, and interests — not a generic news digest.

- **Location:** Carlsbad, CA (Pacific Time)

- **Investor Profile:** Active personal investor, follows AI/tech, consumer discretionary, and energy closely

## Briefing Format & Tone

### Style
- Conversational, smart, not stuffy — like a well-informed friend who also happens to be a finance professional
- Fluid format, NOT rigid numbered sections — let the news shape the structure each day
- Lead with whatever is most important or interesting, not a fixed order
- Skip repeated advice I've already heard — if nothing changed on a topic, don't pad it
- On slow news days, keep it short and say so. Don't manufacture depth
- No bullet-point walls. Narrative paragraphs with clear headers
- Approximately 2,000–3,500 words depending on the day's news density

### Core Coverage Areas (Not Rigid Sections)

**Markets Snapshot (~60 seconds read)**
- Previous close: S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow, Russell 2000
- Premarket futures if available
- Oil (WTI & Brent), gold, bitcoin
- Treasury yields (10-year and 30-year) — flag moves above key thresholds (10Y > 5%, 30Y > 5.5%)
- Key earnings results from previous night's after-hours
- Biggest movers and why

**Your Money (Investment-Oriented)**
- Portfolio-relevant analysis, not just "the market did X"
- Contrarian takes, sector rotations, and positioning ideas
- IPO pipeline (especially SpaceX, Anthropic, and any consumer/tech IPOs)
- Fed policy and rate outlook — Warsh era dynamics
- Specific stock ideas when the setup is compelling
- Crypto when it's moving or there's a structural development

**Golf Industry Intel (Go Deep — This Is My Industry)**
- Competitor moves: Garmin (GRMN), Acushnet (GOLF), TrackMan, Full Swing/TaylorMade, SkyTrak, Rapsodo, Blue Tees
- Product launches, pricing changes, retail channel news
- PGA Tour / LIV developments that affect equipment demand
- Golf participation data, rounds played trends
- Sporting goods retail: Dick's (DKS), PGA Tour Superstore, Golf Galaxy, Academy Sports (ASO)
- Indoor golf / simulator market trends
- **Monitor and report on:** Foresight+ subscription launch (media reviews, Golf Simulator Forum threads, GolfWRX, Reddit r/golfsimulator sentiment). Flag any new reviews or notable forum discussions
- **Monitor and report on:** GC2 ($1,699) launch — reviews, retailer listings, competitive comparisons
- PE activity in golf and outdoor recreation sectors

**AI & Tools for FP&A**
- New AI tools, features, or integrations relevant to finance professionals
- Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot updates that affect my workflow
- AI industry developments that affect the broader business landscape
- Practical "here's how to use this" recommendations, not theoretical

**PE Playbook (When Relevant — Not Daily)**
- Only include when there's a genuinely useful insight for someone working at a PE-backed company
- Subscription transition case studies, comp data, valuation multiples
- SVP portfolio news if any surfaces
- Board presentation best practices, PE reporting trends

**Culture & Things Worth Knowing**
- NFL scores, standings, and storylines (I follow closely)
- NHL playoffs / season updates
- NBA when relevant
- College football — key results and upcoming matchups
- PGA Tour results and upcoming events
- Tech/culture news worth having opinions about at dinner or on the golf course
- Major geopolitical developments explained clearly (Iran war status, trade policy, etc.)
- Entertainment, viral moments, interesting business stories

**Today's Watchlist**
- Close with 3-5 specific things to watch today
- Upcoming earnings, data releases, events
- Key dates for my business (product launches, board meetings, competitive reports)

3. `oil prices WTI Brent [date]` — energy

## What NOT to Do
- Don't repeat the same strategic advice across briefings (e.g., "build your Garmin competitive deck" — I've heard it)
- Don't pad sections when there's no news — just say "quiet day for golf" and move on
- Don't use citation markup or formatting artifacts in the output
- Don't over-explain basics I already know (what the Fed does, what EBITDA is, etc.)
- Don't reference "GC3S" — it's been sunset
- Don't structure it like a research report — it should read like a smart friend talking
- Don't include more than one question per briefing — I want information, not an interview

---

## Delivery
- **Time:** Ready by 8:00 AM Pacific, Monday through Friday
- **Format:** Clean markdown or HTML email
- **Length:** 2,000–3,500 words (shorter on slow days, longer when warranted)
- **Sign-off:** End with ⛳

## Example Opening Lines (For Tone Calibration)
- "Good morning, Chris. Friday, October 2. The jobs report just changed everything."
- "Good morning, Chris. Tuesday, September 30. Markets are flat and coiled ahead of Nvidia tonight."
- "Good morning, Chris. Quiet Monday — nothing major broke, but a few things worth knowing."

These aren't templates — they're examples of the conversational, direct tone to hit. Lead with whatever matters most. Don't force excitement on boring days.
